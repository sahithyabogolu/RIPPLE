# RIPPLE

### Know your cash. Know what to do next.

RIPPLE is an AI-assisted financial decision-support platform that turns scattered financial files into a clearer view of cash, upcoming obligations, liquidity pressure, and possible next steps.

Designed for individuals and businesses, RIPPLE helps users move from spreadsheet confusion to informed cash-flow decisions.

## Explore RIPPLE

Upload an Excel or CSV file to understand your financial position, identify potential cash-flow pressure, and explore practical next steps.

**[Try RIPPLE](https://sahithyabogolu.github.io/RIPPLE/)**

## What RIPPLE Helps You Understand

- Available cash and expected inflows
- Upcoming bills and financial obligations
- Potential liquidity pressure
- Missing or unclear financial information
- Recommended collection, payment, or protection actions
- Possible outcomes through simple scenarios
- The figures supporting each recommendation

## Designed Around One Question

> **What should I collect, pay, delay, or protect next?**

RIPPLE focuses on the timing of cash—not just historical financial reporting.

## Built for Clarity

RIPPLE combines AI-assisted development, browser-based processing, and transparent financial logic to make financial information easier to understand and act on.

## Technology

HTML · CSS · Vanilla JavaScript · SheetJS · GitHub Pages

## Decision-Support Notice

RIPPLE provides financial summaries, calculations, scenario estimates, and suggested actions based on uploaded information. It supports financial review and planning but does not replace professional financial advice.

*RIPPLE is currently available as an early-access prototype.*

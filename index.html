DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy & Security

How your data is handled

  • Uploaded files are processed locally in your browser in this version.
  • Your financial records are not intentionally transmitted to a remote server by this application.
  • Analysis history is stored only in your browser when local storage is enabled.
  • Do not upload passwords, payment-card details, authentication tokens, government identification documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Clear your browser storage or use the New Analysis function when working on a shared computer.
  • Local browser processing does not replace an organisation’s formal information-security, access-control, retention, or regulatory requirements.
